Experimental Design: The study was an observational comparison between two orchard sites representing contrasting orchard structures. 
Orchard system was not randomized or replicated, 
and differences between LD and MD may therefore include both orchard-system and site-specific effects.

Equipment used in both orchards
-------------------------------
EC system manufacturer and model: LI- 7500 DS open-path CO2/H2O analyzers (LI-COR, Lincoln, NE, USA)
sonic anemometer model: Gill WindMaster Pro sonic anemometers (Lymington, Hampshire, United Kingdom)
open-path configuration
sampling frequency: 10 Hz
data-logging system: SmartFlux 3 (LI_COR) 
coordinate rotation method: Planar-fit
averaging interval: 30 minutes
installation: LD installed January 1, 2023. MD installed August 1, 2023
removal: March 1, 2026
calibration frequency: every 6 months

NEE is reported in g C m⁻² over the specified aggregation period. 
Negative NEE indicates net ecosystem carbon uptake, and positive NEE indicates net carbon release to the atmosphere.
