Figure 2. Cumulative growing-season precipitation measured at the low-density (LD) orchard eddy-covariance tower in 2024 and 2025. Curves begin at budbreak on 28 March 2024 and 15 April 2025 and extend through 31 October. Total growing-season precipitation was 351 mm in 2024 and 439 mm in 2025. Although precipitation accumulated more slowly during much of June through mid-August 2025, several late-season rainfall events resulted in the greater 2025 total.

Figure 3. Chained cumulative net ecosystem carbon balance (NECB; upper panel) and weekly differences in net ecosystem exchange (NEE; lower panel) for the low-density (LD) and moderate-density (MD) apple orchards during 2024 and 2025. Cumulative NECB was calculated by continuously accumulating daily NEE across both years and adding harvested-fruit carbon as an ecosystem export at each harvest, indicated by the vertical dotted lines. The 2025 trajectories therefore begin from the ending 2024 carbon balances rather than resetting to zero. Negative cumulative NECB values indicate net carbon retention within the orchard ecosystem. Gray shading denotes the growing seasons. Lower-panel bars represent weekly MD − LD NEE differences. Positive values indicate greater carbon uptake or lower CO₂ release by LD, whereas negative values indicate greater carbon uptake or lower CO₂ release by MD. Dashed horizontal lines indicate zero.
